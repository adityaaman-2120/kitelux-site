About Kitelux

An independent software studio in Kolkata, India

Who we are

Kitelux is an independent software studio based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India. Kitelux is the trading name of Aditya Aman, an individual developer.

Today Kitelux has one product: KiteVault, an offline-first password manager for Android, currently in testing.

Mission

To build small, focused apps that work offline and respect your privacy, and to be honest about what they can and cannot protect.

What we will never do

  • Show you ads.
  • Sell, rent or trade your data.
  • Track you with analytics, trackers or third-party advertising services.
