What it is

KiteVault is an offline-first password manager for Android. The logins, notes, folders, 2FA secrets and settings you put into it are saved in a single encrypted file in the app's private storage on your phone. Kitelux runs no servers that receive your vault.

Features

Encrypted vault

Everything is stored in one encrypted file on your phone, never on Kitelux servers.

Biometric unlock

Optional fingerprint or face unlock, handled by Android. KiteVault never receives or stores your biometric data.

Autofill

Optional. Enable KiteVault as your Android autofill provider to get matching logins in apps and browsers. Matching happens on the device.

TOTP 2FA with QR scan

Scan a 2FA QR code with the camera when you add one. Images are processed on the device and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Password breach check

Optional check against Have I Been Pwned using k-anonymity: only the first 5 characters of a password's SHA-1 hash are sent.

Optional Google Drive backup

Upload your already-encrypted vault to the hidden drive.appdata folder in your own Google Drive. The data goes directly between your phone and Google.

Export

Export your vault to a file, for example CSV or PDF. Exported files are not encrypted.

Clipboard auto-clear

When you copy a password or code, KiteVault clears the clipboard automatically after a short delay.

Security model

  • Your vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • The encryption key is derived from your master password with Argon2id.
  • Your master password is never stored, anywhere, and never leaves your phone.
  • Kitelux has no servers that receive your vault or your data.
  • We cannot recover your vault. If you forget your master password and have no recovery key, nobody can open it, including us.
  • Decrypted data is held in memory only while the vault is unlocked.

We use vetted, widely reviewed cryptographic libraries and never custom cryptography.

What it cannot protect against

No software is perfectly secure. Be aware of these limits.

  • A rooted or malware-infected phone. A strong master password, a locked phone and an up-to-date Android are important parts of your protection.
  • A forgotten master password without a recovery key. The vault cannot be opened by anyone.
  • Unencrypted exports. A file you export (for example CSV or PDF) is not encrypted; you choose where it goes and you are responsible for it.

Frequently asked questions

Does KiteVault collect my data?

No. Kitelux does not collect any personal data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Your master password is never stored, and we cannot recover your data. If you have no recovery key, the vault cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

Does KiteVault use the internet?

Only for two optional features: Google Drive backup and the password breach check. There are no other network calls.

What is sent when I run the password breach check?

Your password is hashed (SHA-1) on your phone and only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to the Have I Been Pwned service. Your password, its full hash and which entry it belongs to are never sent. As with any internet request, the service can see your IP address.

Are exported files encrypted?

No. If you export your vault to a file (for example CSV or PDF), that file is not encrypted. You choose where it goes and you are responsible for it.
