Our principles

Offline first

Your data lives on your device. Network features are optional and off by default.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no crash-report services, no third-party trackers.

Vetted cryptography

We use well-audited libraries. We never write our own cryptography.

Honest about limits

We tell you what we can't protect against, like a compromised phone or a forgotten master password.
